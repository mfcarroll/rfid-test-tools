Chameleon Ultra field measurement (experimental)

Point the CU at a reader instead of a card, and find out what kind of field the reader makes. On LF, the CU measures the carrier's frequency and looks for the short gaps a reader leaves in its field when it sends a command. On HF, it counts the ISO 14443-A polls the reader sends. The CU logs each field, so you can take it to a reader on battery and read the results here afterwards.

Everything happens in this browser. The page talks to the CU over USB. Nothing is sent to any server, and it doesn't save anything unless you press Copy or Download.

While measuring, the CU doesn't emulate any card. No slot answers a reader, and the buttons only wake the CU. The experimental firmware starts with measurement on. Turn it off here when you're done, or flash stock firmware.

Connect the Chameleon Ultra

This page requires Chrome or Edge on a computer. Use USB, not Bluetooth, and close anything else using the CU, such as the CU app or the Python CLI.

Checking the firmware…

This firmware has no field measurement. Flash the experimental firmware below, then connect again.

1. Check measurement is on

The experimental firmware starts with measurement on. It stays on through sleep, unplugging and reconnecting, until you turn it off. It goes off if the battery runs flat.

2. Take it to a reader

Unplug the CU and hold its button side flat against the reader for about 5 seconds, then take it away. The field LED and a cyan light show an LF field being measured; green shows an HF field. A red flash means the reader's field dropped for a moment. Some readers switch their field off all the time, and some only weaken it. The report says which. You can visit more than one reader, but the log keeps only the last 12 LF measurements, and each reader's field is easier to read on its own.

Hitag readers: check which mode they use. Clear the log in the browser before you go. At the reader, hold the CU against it, then bring your fob up next to it and keep both there for about 5 seconds. Take them both away, wait until the CU's lights go off, and do it again, three times in all. Then read the log as usual.

With the CU still plugged in, you can hold it against a reader and press Measure now instead. That measures LF once, straight away.

3. Read the results

Plug the CU back in, connect, and read the log. Read it before you flash anything, because a flash clears it. Clear it before measuring a different reader.

Advanced: envelope mode and raw captures

The envelope capture looks for the reader's gaps. With the load on (the default), the CU's detector follows the field quickly. With it off, the detector holds the field's level and short gaps don't show. With the load off or the tone, the report doesn't look for the reader's commands, and only a field that stays on gets a carrier frequency, from the load tone.

A raw capture records LF_RSSI with the CU held against a reader, for analysis elsewhere. The samples can include a reader's commands, so treat the download as private.

Firmware

Field measurement needs the experimental firmware: the card search page's firmware (lf-search) with field measurement added (source). Flashing replaces the CU's firmware and keeps the current slots.

          The carrier frequency is measured against the CU's own crystal, which
