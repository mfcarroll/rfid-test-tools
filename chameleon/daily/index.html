Chameleon Ultra daily firmware

The firmware I run on my own Chameleon Ultras: upstream firmware with the fixes and features below, most of them waiting in pull requests. It updates whenever I update mine.

This is unofficial firmware. It works with the Chameleon Ultra only, not the Lite. Flashing keeps your slots and saved cards. To go back, flash stock v2.3.0 from "Other firmware".

    Flash it

    Plug your CU in to USB, not Bluetooth, and close anything else using it, such as the CU app or the Python CLI. This page needs Chrome or Edge on a computer. The browser asks you to pick the CU when you flash.
