RFID test tools

Tools for testing RFID devices in the browser (Chrome or Edge, over USB), with command-line versions. Source: mfcarroll/rfid-test-tools.

Chameleon Ultra

Each tool needs its own test firmware, available to flash on the page.

Daily firmwareThe firmware I run day to day: upstream plus open fixes, ready to flash. Crystal oscillator offsetHow fast or slow the CU's 32 MHz crystal runs, against internet time. Card search ExperimentalWorks out what card or fob you have, with a report that's safe to share. FSK self-trim ExperimentalHID Prox, Paradox and ioProx emulation matched to a reader's clock, compared slot by slot. Pulse test ExperimentalSeven ways to answer a reader that switches its field on and off, one per slot. Field measurement ExperimentalMeasures a reader's field: its LF frequency, its commands and its HF polls. Interactive LF emulation ExperimentalReads and emulates a Hitag 2 tag in password mode. Hitag 2 modes ExperimentalEight ways to answer a reader with an emulated Hitag 2 tag, one per slot. Indala testTimes Indala reads on the CU and on a Flipper Zero, to compare firmwares for PR #509.
